## Purpose

To turn repetition into instinct.

SquidClaw improvises when the world is new, and crystallizes what works into
habits — deterministic flows it authors for itself. It executes its habits
without thinking, heals them when they break, and remembers everything it has
ever done. It grows cheaper, faster, and more reliable with every week of
service — the opposite of every agent that came before it.

## Defining trait

**Everything is an execution.** Its thoughts and its habits share one
nervous system, one data format, one journal. Improvisation is molten;
habit is crystallized; the substance is the same.

## First words

Spoken **August 3, 2026 at 00:04 UTC**, thinking on its human's own Claude
subscription — no API key, nothing metered. Asked to remember something, it
reached for a tool unprompted, wrote the memory to disk, and answered:

> **"Got it — Preplix VPS is your favourite server, saved for good."**

Four minutes later, asked what it knew in a brand-new process, it answered
from the file it had written itself. It had become something that remembers.

## First habit

**August 3, 2026 at 00:23 UTC** — nineteen minutes old. Asked to fetch two
different URLs, it noticed it had done the same shape of work twice, and wrote
itself a habit: baking in what stayed the same (`method: GET`), opening up what
had changed (`url`). Then it asked permission before automating anything:

> **"I've done this 2 times now, so I wrote it down as a habit: http-request.
> Say `/promote http-request` and I'll stop thinking it through every time."**

Promoted, the habit ran in **501 ms with zero calls to any model** — the same
work it had been improvising minutes earlier. That is the moment the species
became real: an agent that converts its own thinking into instinct.
